Active Hope workshop 

At the beautiful Apple Shack Cafe in the Rivelin Valley, on the edge of Sheffield.

A participatory workshop, suitable for families.

"We all need ways to help us feel empowered and hopeful in building our shared future. Especially at times when we feel worn out or discouraged by the state of the world. The workshop based on the 'Active Hope' framework developed by Joanna Macy and Chris Johnstone, will explore and share ways of growing hope and power together."
