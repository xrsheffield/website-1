Please come and help print things for the [Peace & Craft Fair](https://xrsheffield.org/events/peace-craft-fair/) (the following day) and finish making flags for marches & demos.  No special skills required, just come along, see what others are doing and join in.  A great chance to get to know people while helping with the struggle against climate change.  Tea, coffee and snacks available (or please bring snacks to share).

If you have any spare t-shirts or scraps of plain coloured material (or can find any going cheap in a charity shop) please bring them along to print and/or use on our stall at the Peace & Craft Fair.

No need to come for the whole three hours, just drop in any time between 6 and 9pm :)

### Where?
At [KIAC](http://www.kiac-sheffield.org/contact-us.html) (Kelham Island Arts Collective), 40 Ball Street, Sheffield S3 8DB

Note - KIAC has two entrances - please use the one with a white door near the corner of Ball Street and Mowbray Street
