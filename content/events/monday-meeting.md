A special Monday meeting to welcome new members and debrief from the COP28 events

(Tea and biscuits provided)

After a brief introduction for everyone, those new to XR can learn about the XR Sheffield community and XR's principles, values and ways of working together

For those already familiar with XR Sheffield, there will be a chance for a facilitated debrief from COP28 events including Saturday's Climate Justice March

After the separate sessions we will come together we will share ideas and think about plans for the coming year

At the end there will be time for people to get to know one another, socialise and enjoy shared snacks (please bring a contribution if you like)
