Please come and help create banners and placards for the 5th Anniversary of Sheffield Council's declaration of a Climate Emergancy.  No special skills required, just come along, see what others are doing and join in.  A great chance to get to know people while helping with the struggle against climate change.  Tea, coffee and snacks available (or please bring snacks to share).

No need to come for the whole three hours, just drop in any time between 6 and 9pm :)

### Where?
At [KIAC](http://www.kiac-sheffield.org/contact-us.html) (Kelham Island Arts Collective), 40 Ball Street, Sheffield S3 8DB

Note - KIAC has two entrances - please use the one with a white door near the corner of Ball Street and Mowbray Street
