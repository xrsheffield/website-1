🎨 ART WORKSHOP 🎨

Please come and help make fishes’ heads (for the March For Clean Water), flags, banners, placards or anything else needed for upcoming actions 

No special skills required.  There will be tea, coffee & nibbles available (please bring snacks to share if you like)

Note: there are 2 entrances to KIAC on Ball Street.  Please use the entrance with a white door near Mowbray St, go up the stairs, turn left and follow the signs to Pottery/Drawing. 

Drop in any time between 6 and 8pm.  All welcome.
