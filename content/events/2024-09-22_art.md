🎨 ART WORKSHOP 🎨

Please come and help paint words on hi-vis vests for roles at the Reclaim the Power Camp.  There are quite a few to do, so the more people the better.  No special skills required.  There will be tea, coffee & nibbles available (please bring snacks to share if you like).

Note: there are 2 entrances to KIAC on Ball Street.  Please use the entrance with a white door near Mowbray St, go up the stairs, turn left and follow the signs to Pottery/Drawing. 

Drop in any time between 6 and 9pm.  All welcome.
