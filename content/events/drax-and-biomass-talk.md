Organised by [People and Planet Society](https://su.sheffield.ac.uk/activities/view/people-and-planet-society). Open to non Uni of Sheffield people as well. Speakers from Biomass Watch, Axe Drax and Reclaim the Power

**Drax Greenwashing and Biomass**

Join BioFuel Watch for an eye-opening event delving into the controversial topic of biomass greenwashing. Discover the truth behind biomass and learn about the real environmental impact. BioFuel Watch will break down the context around the Yorkshire-based Drax power station and its current impacts.

This in-person event will be held in the Gallery Eye Area on Level 4 of the student union, providing an opportunity for learning and lively discussions on the false climate solution that is Biomass power and what we can do about it!

This event is open to all, so bring your friends along for an informative and engaging evening.
