Demonstration and Rally on Wednesday 1st February as part of TUC's national day of action to protect the right strike. Assemble 12 noon at Devonshire Green. At 12:30 the demonstration sets of through Sheffield. At 1pm the rally will gather at the City Hall steps. 

Bring your XR flags, show solidarity with all Striking workers and defend the right to strike!
