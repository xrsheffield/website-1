Sheffield Climate Vigil - 1st Monday of every month at 5-6pm at the top of Howard St, and 3rd Monday of every month at 12-1pm outside the town hall.  Anyone can just turn up, be peaceful and hold a placard (placards provided), or can do leafletting - engage in climate conversations with the public if they want.

[Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/480149003818269/)  

### Where?

Outside the Town Hall
