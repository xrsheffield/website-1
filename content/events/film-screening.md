XR Sheffield and South Yorkshire Climate Alliance are hosting a screening of **'Once You Know'** followed by a facilitated discussion.

Showing as part of Special screenings & events

Once You Know is a feature documentary which explores the possibility of climate-induced collapse, our dismay in the face of uncertainty, and ways to adapt to unavoidable changes while moving towards a desirable future.

This special screening will be followed by an interactive facilitated discussion, based on The Work that Reconnects, a process developed by eco-psychologist Joanna Macy. The audience will be led through different stages of processing the deep issues raised by the film, experiencing a beneficial exchange in which people are invited to share their experiences, with the aim of turning the public into a ‘community in motion’, inspired to take action.
