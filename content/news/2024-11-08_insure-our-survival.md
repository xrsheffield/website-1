Insure our Survival - That's a really brilliant idea!

Large commercial insurers and brokers have the potential to slow down or stop fossil fuel projects because without insurance they cannot go ahead. This huge national initiative was extremely well supported by Sheffield XR and Five Rivers Rising samba fusion band. Some went to London, some did an action in Sheffield, some went to Manchester, some went to Birmingham, ... and some attended several of these events!
