On Saturday morning Sheffield XR conducted a survey asking people’s views on the climate crisis after the weekly Park Run in Endcliffe Park.

Three questions were asked:

1. How concerned are you about the climate crisis?
2. Do you think our government is doing enough about it?
3. Would you consider going to parliament alongside 100,000 from April 21st, 2023?
