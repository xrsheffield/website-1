On Wednesday we targeted the Insurance Broker AON at its office off Ecclesall Rd as part of a national week of action against insurance companies and brokers insuring climate chaos

AON have “deep experience across all segments of the natural resources industry—including mining, oil and gas, power generation, renewables, and utilities" - their own website

New oil and gas facilities threaten our survival - we call on AON and others to commit to ending these ties!

Insurance company Zurich just dropped new oil and gas - why can't AON do the same?
