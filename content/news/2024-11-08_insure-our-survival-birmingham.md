How much more flooding do we need?
Following on from the Manchester Action, 8 drummers from Sheffield XR's Five Rivers Rising joined the Rhythms band for the Birmingham Action.

 

The large band provided a powerful, primal beat as the march weaved its way around the city targeting the offices of Tokio Marine, Hiscox, Howden, AIG, Chubb and Allianz.
