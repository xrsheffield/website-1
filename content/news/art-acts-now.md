Calling all creators! We are seeking small to medium sized 2D artworks for an exhibition in the Café area of The Art House on the following themes:


1. Heading for extinction - Identifying the crisis
2. Visioning a better future - There is hope


We want to communicate how these themes would impact locally to show the closeness of the climate emergency. Art is a great communicator to provoke ideas and discussions. We welcome all involvement! The links below may serve as useful prompts to support ideas around these themes:

https://www.theguardian.com/…/worst-case-scenario-2050-clim…

https://www.unlockingsustainablecities.org/home.html

https://www.robhopkins.net/…/james-mckay-the-man-who-draws…/

https://lowcarbon.leeds.ac.uk/dreams-of-a-low-carbon-future/

If you are interested in submitting some of your work please direct message us on here, Twitter or Instagram - We will then let you know where you can share it!

Alternatively, email xrsheffieldart@gmail.comPlease ensure that it is in JPEG form and add framed dimensions if work is framed.We will make a selection from the submissions and if your work is selected may ask you to print and frame the image - If you cannot do this we can arrange this for you. We will inform you when and where to deliver the artwork closer to the time.
